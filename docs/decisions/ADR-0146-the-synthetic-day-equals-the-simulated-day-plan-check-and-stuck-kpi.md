# ADR-0146 · 2026-10-15 · The synthetic day equals the simulated day; plan feasibility check and stuck-agent KPI

- **Status:** active (the 30 h bound and the plan check are implemented on branch `feature/i443-plan-bound-and-check`; the stuck-agent KPI is decided here and implemented in the stuck-agent analysis (issue #443, part 2); the behaviour effect is NOT yet measured, the 1 % A/B run with MATSim iterations is owed and will add the run manifest)
- **Numbering:** ADR-0146 is the next free id after ADR-0145. Checked on 2026-10-16 across all local and remote branches: the highest record anywhere is ADR-0145 (outside-tour modes, on the sibling branch of the same plan); ADR-0146 is not used on any branch.
- **Issue:** TUBS-IVS/eqasim-bs#443 (plan feasibility check, stuck-agent KPI, plan-time bound). The mode feature that removes the main cause of the late plan ends is #453 and ADR-0145.
- **Relates to:** ADR-0145 (outside-tour modes: the frozen tours whose diary mode the cutter turns into late end times), ADR-0141 (portal trips, which introduced the `outside` stays the cutter times), ADR-0108 (the home closure and its plan-time cap, which this decision moves to the new bound), ADR-0136 (the stage token hashes its whole import closure).

## Context

- **Measurements of the final iteration of the 100 % run `prod100_20260925`.** Taken on felix in iteration 92 with the scripts in `~/prod100_20260925/stuck_analysis/` and recorded in issue #443; they are findings about one run, not validation:
  - 21,722 plans end after 30:00 in the input population of that run. 15,652 of them have a diary (the pipeline trip table) that ends before 30:00 and 15,613 of those contain an `outside` activity: the late end is made by the cutter, not by the diary (mechanism and the frozen tours in ADR-0145).
  - What remains beyond that. Three figures with DIFFERENT populations and definitions, all felix measurements recorded in the issue #443 comment and not committed; no arithmetic relation between them is claimed and they must not be added or compared: 5,895 and 2,446 are persons of the pipeline trip table (before the population writer, the cut and the freight injection), counted by cause: 5,895 are late only through the synthetic home closure (the closure dwell is drawn against a 36 h bound), 2,446 through midnight-shifted diaries (the donor diary itself is late). 7,924 is a band of the INPUT plans of the run (the written population, after the cut): plans whose end falls between 30 and 36 h, which is 0.66 % of the persons at 100 %; that band can contain plans of either cause and plans whose late end the cutter made.
  - 29,645 residents are stuck at the end of the run (stuck rate 14.7 % with a tour touching `outside`, 0.9 % without; 73.6 % of the stuck residents have such a tour). 20,823 are stuck at the simulation end, 8,822 during the day.
- **The mismatch.** `braunschweig.popsim.plan_validation.MAX_PLAN_TIME_SECONDS` was 36 h while the simulation ends at 30:00: `org.eqasim.core.scenario.config.GenerateConfig` sets `qsim.endTime` to 30:00. A synthetic chain that ends between 30 and 36 h passed the validation and then cannot be lived in the simulated day; the agent is stuck at the simulation end. Nothing between the population writer and MATSim checked whether a plan fits the simulated day.
- **Nothing made the defects visible per run.** eqasim writes `stuck_analysis.csv` (a count per iteration); it does not say who is stuck, when, in which mode, and whether the person's tour is one the mode choice can repair.

## Decision

1. **The synthetic day equals the simulated day (30 h).** `braunschweig.popsim.plan_validation.MAX_PLAN_TIME_SECONDS` is `30 * 3600`, equal to `qsim.endTime` written by `GenerateConfig`; it is the ONE constant for the plan-time bound. No Java change. Consequences, all through the existing constant:
   - the empirical home closure dwell is capped at the bound with its `HOME_CLOSURE_DWELL_S` floor (ADR-0108, ruling R19 stays: the constant `fixed_1h` path is never capped, a constant-path chain beyond the bound goes to the resample as before);
   - `trips_stage._assert_time_bound` (bound plus jitter), the time-imputation pools and the departure-time model guards (`departure_time_model`, last arrival at most the bound) use the new value;
   - a chain whose repaired times still exceed the bound (midnight-shifted diaries and multi-shift chains) is resampled from a same-cell donor. The resample share is logged; a WARN fires above the module constant `BOUND_RESAMPLE_WARN_RATE` (0.01).
2. **A plan check after the cut.** `braunschweig/matsim/simulation/plan_check.py`, called as the last step of `braunschweig.matsim.simulation.prepare.execute` after the cut, the freight injection and the fare inputs, so the checked file is the one MATSim reads. It streams the written population (located through the written config's `plans.inputPlansFile`, never an assumed file name) and counts, per subpopulation and per class:
   - plans with an activity END TIME after the simulated day (`n_plans_late`); plans whose LAST PLANNED ARRIVAL (the last leg's departure, the end time of the previous activity, plus the leg's `trav_time`) is after the simulated day (`n_plans_arrival_late`, report only, not part of the failure rule); plans with non-monotonic end times; walk legs over `walk_max_distance_m`; bicycle legs over `bicycle_max_distance_m` (straight-line between the adjacent activities); legs with a travel time over `leg_max_duration_s`. A plan with more legs than activities - 1 is a corrupt population and raises `ValueError` naming the person.
   - The classes are `frozen` (the plan, or for leg metrics the leg's own home-based tour, touches an `outside` activity: the tours the discrete mode choice never re-chooses, `OutsideFilter`) and `in_region` (the mode choice's job, informational).
   - It asserts `qsim.endTime` of the written config equals `MAX_PLAN_TIME_SECONDS` and raises on a difference (one constant, no silent drift between synthesis and simulation).
   - When, for RESIDENTS in the FROZEN class, the share of late plans (activity end time after the day) or of non-monotonic plans exceeds `braunschweig.plan_check.max_frozen_defect_share`, it logs at ERROR with the shares and CONTINUES (`braunschweig.plan_check.fail_on_threshold` false, the default and the production value) or raises `ValueError` (true). The threshold is an ASSUMPTION; it becomes a failure only after the A/B of #443 has calibrated it, because an uncalibrated threshold must not abort a production run (ruling R22). The in-region figures and freight are reported, never failed on (the mode choice repairs the former, issue #444 owns the freight defects). The rates are logged under `[plan_check]`; `plan_check.json` in the stage directory carries the counts, the shares, and a `threshold` block (`max_frozen_defect_share`, `fail_on_threshold`, `exceeded`), and is written BEFORE the check can raise.
   - Config keys (defaults in `plan_check.py`, production values in `configs/base_bs.yml`): `braunschweig.plan_check.enabled`, `.walk_max_distance_m`, `.bicycle_max_distance_m`, `.leg_max_duration_s`, `.max_frozen_defect_share`, `.fail_on_threshold`.
3. **A stuck-agent KPI from the final iteration (decided here; the code arrives with the stuck-agent analysis of issue #443, part 2).** The KPI is a SUB-ANALYSIS of the analysis suite (`braunschweig.analysis.stuck_agents`, run by `analysis_suite`), NOT a separate cached stage. It streams the `stuckAndAbort` events of the final iteration's `output_events.xml.gz`, joins the subpopulation (`output_persons.csv.gz`) and the frozen flag (the person's selected plan in `output_plans.xml.gz` contains an `outside` activity), and writes a per-agent table and a summary under the suite's output directory. The categories are: stuck at the simulation end (event time at or after the simulation end, no leg mode) versus stuck during the day, by mode, subpopulation and frozen flag. The simulation end is READ FROM THE RUN'S OUTPUT CONFIG (`qsim.endTime` of `output_config.xml`, else `output_config_reduced.xml`), not assumed from the constant; only when neither file carries it does the analysis fall back to `MAX_PLAN_TIME_SECONDS`, and then logs a WARNING naming the files. The dashboard's run metrics gain an additive `stuck` block from the summary, and the analysis logs a WARN when the resident stuck share exceeds the configurable warn share `analysis_stuck_warn_share` (default 0.01, ASSUMPTION below; the key is declared by the analysis suite). eqasim's `stuck_analysis.csv` stays as it is. Until that code exists the KPI is a decision, not a measurement.
4. **Every A/B metric is read from the LAST iteration.** Iteration 0 is the router's first guess, not the equilibrium; the A/B runs with iterations until the eqasim termination criterion and reads the stuck categories, the plan-check figures and the mode shares from the final iteration. This is stability of the mode shares, not validation (the termination criterion has no real-world reference).

### Rejected alternatives

- **Extend the simulation to 36 h** (user decision 2026-10-15). It would simulate 0.66 % of the agents (the 7,924 plans between 30 and 36 h) on an empty next-morning network without public transport; the cost is a longer simulated day for every agent and a fake second morning for a small tail. Capping the synthesis at the simulated day keeps the simulated day as it is and puts the correction where the defect is made.
- **A Java `StuckAnalysisHandler` extension.** The Python stream over the final iteration's events costs minutes and joins the frozen flag and the subpopulation from files that already exist; a handler change would be a Java change in the eqasim fork for what the analysis stage does without one.
- **Failing the plan check on in-region defects.** The discrete mode choice re-chooses in-region tours and repairs their modes and times during the iterations; a defect in the input of an in-region tour is not a defect of the model. The check therefore raises on the frozen resident class only and reports the rest.

### Assumptions (stated, not measured)

- ASSUMPTION: `braunschweig.plan_check.walk_max_distance_m` 5,000 m. Anchored in the committed SrV table of ADR-0145 (`eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_mode_by_distance_band.csv`): the walk share is NOT zero beyond 5 km straight-line but below 1 % in every group and band, with one exception: work at 5 to 10 km is 0.16 %, education at 5 to 10 km 0.08 %, the pooled group 0.68 %, and the group `other` is 1.05 % at 5 to 10 km (0.98 % at 20 to 30 km). It is a threshold for counting legs, not a reference value.
- ASSUMPTION: `braunschweig.plan_check.bicycle_max_distance_m` 30,000 m, resting on thin evidence. In the same table the work bicycle share at 30 to 45 km is 0.064 %, which is a SINGLE unweighted trip (`n_cell` 1 of 666 trips in the band); the group `other` is still 0.68 % at 20 to 30 km (7 of 603 trips) and 0.95 % at 30 to 45 km (4 of 330). 30 km is a choice for counting legs, not a measured limit.
- ASSUMPTION: `braunschweig.plan_check.leg_max_duration_s` 10,800 s (3 h): a single leg of more than three hours is not a plausible trip of one regional day.
- ASSUMPTION: `braunschweig.plan_check.max_frozen_defect_share` 0.005: the share of frozen resident plans that may be late (activity end time after the day) or non-monotonic before the check reports an ERROR. It is a choice; the A/B calibrates it. `braunschweig.plan_check.fail_on_threshold` stays false (the check logs and continues) until the A/B has calibrated this threshold; setting it true is a later decision with its own record.
- ASSUMPTION: `BOUND_RESAMPLE_WARN_RATE` 0.01 (a constant in `plan_validation`, like `CLOSURE_CAP_WARN_RATE`): above 1 % of the persons resampled for the bound, the bound and the donor pool disagree structurally. The measured share was 0.2 % with the former 36 h bound; it is not known for 30 h before the run.
- ASSUMPTION: the resident stuck warn share 0.01 of decision 3 is a choice until a measured stuck distribution exists.

### Limitations

- **The check evaluates PLANNED times, not routed ones.** `n_plans_arrival_late` uses the `trav_time` written in the plan (the diary's travel time). A plan whose planned last arrival is near 30:00 can still be stuck at the end of the run when the routed travel time exceeds the diary travel time; the bound deliberately has no margin (user decision: 30 h = `qsim.endTime`), so such agents are possible and are counted by the stuck-agent KPI of decision 3, not by the plan check.
- **The per-person jitter is not active in production (evidence).** The production configuration sets `departure_time_model: srv_mapped` (`configs/base_bs.yml`, key `departure_time_model`; no overlay sets it, the two popsim fixtures `configs/fixtures/config_popsim_open_braunschweig.yml` and `config_smoke_popsim_open_mini.yml` set `eqasim_uniform`). `braunschweig.popsim.trips_stage.run` dispatches through `braunschweig.popsim.departure_time_model.apply_departure_time_model`, which calls `braunschweig.popsim.trips_stage.apply_per_person_jitter` ONLY for the model `eqasim_uniform` (the code default of the key, `DEFAULT_DEPARTURE_TIME_MODEL` in `braunschweig.popsim.stage.config_keys`). With `srv_mapped` the model clips the chain offset so that the last arrival is at most `MAX_PLAN_TIME_SECONDS` (the `upper_bound` of `apply_departure_time_model`); only a chain longer than the bound even at departure 0 stays beyond it and is reported (`n_clip_bound_conflicts`). The jitter margin `JITTER_MAX_SECONDS` (1,800 s) of the backstop `trips_stage._assert_time_bound` therefore does not take effect in production. In a run with `departure_time_model: eqasim_uniform` the jitter can move the last arrival up to `JITTER_MAX_SECONDS` beyond the bound, and the plan check would count those plans as late.
- **Thresholds and the `outside` class are ASSUMPTIONS** (above); the plan check compares nothing with observed behaviour.

## Consequences

- **Results change by decision.** Plans that ended between 30 and 36 h (7,924 input plans at 100 % in the run above, 0.66 %) have their closures capped or their diaries resampled where the synthesis made the late end; the 2,446 midnight-shifted diaries at 100 % (0.2 % of the persons) are resampled from same-cell donors where they exceed 30 h (felix measurements of different populations, see Context; no sum is claimed). This is a behaviour change by decision, not a fix of a validated quantity.
- **Cache.** Every stage from `braunschweig.popsim.trips_stage` downstream recomputes, because `plan_validation` and the modules that import the constant are in the import closure of the stages that read them (ADR-0136); the 1 % A/B budgets a fresh 1 % popsim. The prepare stage's validate token also hashes `braunschweig.popsim.plan_validation`, `braunschweig.popsim.closure_dwell` and `data.hts.hts` (the import closure of `plan_check`); all three already sit upstream of prepare in the DAG, so no recomputation is added.
- **New failure modes of prepare.** `plan_check.run` raises on a `qsim.endTime` that differs from the bound, on a written config without `plans.inputPlansFile`, on a population path that does not exist, on a plan with more legs than activities - 1, and above `max_frozen_defect_share` for frozen residents ONLY when `braunschweig.plan_check.fail_on_threshold` is true (default false: ERROR log, run continues). The check is flag-gated (`braunschweig.plan_check.enabled`, default true).
- **Not a validation.** The check counts defects in the input of the simulation and the KPI counts stuck agents in its output. Neither compares the model with observed behaviour; no observed reference for stuck agents exists.
