# ADR-0145 · 2026-10-15 · Outside-tour modes from SrV after the location choice

- **Status:** active (implemented on branch `feature/i453-outside-tour-modes`; the direction of the effect was measured once at 1 % in the run `i453-i443-ab-1pct-2026-10-16`, which is NOT a validation)
- **Numbering:** ADR-0145 is the next free id. Checked on 2026-10-15 across all local and remote branches: the highest record anywhere is ADR-0144 (freight boundary endpoints).
- **Issue:** TUBS-IVS/eqasim-bs#453 (the mode feature). The late plan ends, the plan check and the stuck KPI that the same findings led to are #443 and are recorded in a companion decision with the second pull request, not here.
- **Relates to:** ADR-0141 (portal trips: their gates are `outside` activities and are judged by this stage), ADR-0102 (SrV 2023 as the regional distance reference, whose extraction conventions the table follows), ADR-0136 (the stage token hashes its whole import closure), ADR-0104 (the reporting-day trips view this stage is stacked on).

## Context

- **Findings of the final iteration of the 100 % run `prod100_20260925`.** Measured on felix in iteration 92 (scripts and outputs in `~/prod100_20260925/stuck_analysis/`; these are findings about one run, not validation):
  - 148,592 residents (12.4 %) have a tour that touches an `outside` activity. Their modes are frozen: there were 0 mode changes between iteration 0 and iteration 92, against 57 % changed trips for in-region trips. Their stuck rate is 14.7 % against 0.9 % for everyone else; 73.6 % of the 29,645 stuck residents have such a tour.
  - In these tours 43.5 % of the 33,162 walk legs exceed 5 km straight-line (in-region after the mode choice: 0.7 %), and the 7,670 bicycle legs have a median of 20.7 km.
  - The eqasim cutter ends an `outside` activity when the routed leg crosses the boundary (`TeleportationTripProcessor`, `NetworkTripProcessor`): departure plus the travel time from the far point to the boundary. A 30 km walk is six hours, so the day ends after 30:00, and when the walk is longer than the stay the end times run backwards. The mode is therefore also the main cause of the late and non-monotonic plan ends that #443 measured (21,722 plans end after 30:00 in the input of that run; 15,652 of them have a diary that ends before 30:00 and 15,613 of those have an outside activity).
  - Where the outside tours come from: 40,916 from an assigned work or education location beyond the cut extent and 40,848 from a secondary location the chain solver placed beyond it; for walk legs over 10 km the secondary class is the larger one (9,066 versus 7,503).
- **The mechanism.** The production discrete mode choice runs `ModelType.Tour` with the tour finder on the activity types `[home, outside]` and the `OutsideFilter` (checked in the code for ADR-0141, decision 6). A tour that touches an `outside` activity is removed from the choice, so its diary mode stays for the whole run. In eqasim France this never matters because `data/hts/egt/filtered.py` drops every donor with a trip outside the region; this model keeps nationwide MiD donors and nationwide secondary candidates (ADR-0027, ADR-0141), so a frozen diary mode can sit at a distance where it is not plausible.
- **Why after the location choice.** Half of the outside tours stem from a secondary location (40,848 of 81,764), so a check before the secondary location choice would see only half of the problem. The check needs the realised locations and therefore runs after them.
- **Correction of a statement in #442.** The portal-trips analysis said that public-transport trips become walks "because far destinations are unreachable". The probe of the final plans does not support it: the lost pt trips are short, their primary location has a median distance of 0.9 km from home and 2 % are over 20 km (felix `pt_reach_probe.txt`). The router prefers walking for these trips; far destinations are not the reason. This ADR supersedes that sentence; the portal decisions of ADR-0141 are unaffected.

## Decision

Behind the flag `braunschweig.outside_tour_modes.enabled` (default on, production value in `configs/base_bs.yml` only):

1. **The diary mode is a start value; only the frozen tours are judged.** In eqasim the mode choice decides the equilibrium. The synthesis supplies a plausible mode only where the mode choice cannot act: tours that touch the outside of the cut extent. In-region tours are left alone.
2. **Plausibility is measured against SrV 2023, not against MiD.** The reference is a committed table of mode shares by purpose group and distance band, extracted from the regional SrV 2023 trips (Braunschweig and Region Braunschweig delivery, GIS-routed lengths) with the conventions of ADR-0102 (`scripts/extract_srv_mode_by_distance_band.py`, builder `braunschweig.calibration.srv_mode_by_distance_band`, committed table `eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_mode_by_distance_band.csv`, data record `srv2023_mode_by_distance_band`).
   - Selection (the trips without a valid GIS length are not a random subset: their mode mix differs from the selected trips, see the limitations of the data record): valid GIS length, non-negative weight (`GEWICHT_W_ZENSUS`), mode `E_HVM_5` in walk, bicycle, car, car passenger, pt; Tuesday to Thursday only (the extractor raises otherwise). Unit is the trip, weighted. The header of the committed file records 58,148 trips, of which 12,174 without a valid GIS length, 47 with an unknown mode, 41 with an excluded purpose, leaving n_selected = 45,942.
   - Purpose groups `work`, `education`, `other` and the pooled group `all`. They come from the destination purpose `V_ZWECK` through `braunschweig.calibration.srv_plan_structure.PURPOSE_BY_V_ZWECK`, the pipeline's own code map, so business trips count as work and other education as education (ruling R1 of the implementation: the first draft excluded them; the map is the same as the pipeline's MiD mapping, which keeps the comparison like with like). Trips to home take the group of the origin purpose. Unknown purposes and home-to-home trips are excluded and counted.
   - Distance is the straight-line equivalent `GIS_LAENGE / 1.3` km (the pipeline's detour factor). Bands are `[0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, inf)` km, labelled `0_5` to `45_plus`.
   - Thin cells: per group, from the top band downwards, a band below 50 unweighted trips is merged into the band below. A merge never reaches the 0-5 km band, because walk is plausible only there; a band left without trips, or a group without trips, raises instead of yielding a degenerate cell. In the committed table only education pools (its bands from 30 km up form `30_plus`, n 54); work, other and all keep the seven bands. The table has 135 data rows (four groups, 7 + 7 + 7 + 6 bands, five modes each).
   - The run-time side (constants, band labels, loader) is a separate module without first-party imports, `braunschweig.calibration.srv_mode_by_distance_band_table`, so that the stage token does not depend on the extraction code (ruling R2).
3. **One rule, no hand-picked thresholds.** A mode is implausible at a distance when its SrV share in the leg's (purpose group, band) cell is below `braunschweig.outside_tour_modes.min_share` (default 0.01, range (0, 1)).
   - The cell of a leg: a leg whose origin or destination purpose is `outside` (a portal leg) takes the group `all` and its top band `45_plus`; a leg into `home` takes the group of its origin purpose; otherwise the group of its destination purpose (`work`, `education`, else `other`). The band is the band of the straight-line distance between the realised locations of the leg.
   - A home-based tour starts at every leg that departs from home. A tour is an outside tour when any leg ends outside the cut polygon or at an `outside` activity (portal gates lie inside the extent by construction, so the purpose test catches them). A point exactly on the polygon boundary counts as outside (`shapely.contains_xy`, ruling R5).
   - If any leg of an outside tour is implausible, ONE substitute mode is drawn for the whole tour. The candidates are the modes that (a) the person may use, (b) have a share of at least `min_share` in the cell of EVERY leg of the tour, and (c) keep the mode class of the tour's portal gate (the next bullet). The weights are the SrV shares of the cell of the tour's longest implausible leg. Condition (b) is ruling R16 of the final review: a draw that looked only at the cell of the longest implausible leg could hand a mode that is plausible for a short leg to a tour that also contains a long leg where that mode is not plausible (a reviewer's probe: home to work on foot over 6 km, then 50 km by car to a shop and home; a bicycle is plausible in the 6 km work cell but has a share of 0.0 in the 50 km cell, and it was drawn). When no mode satisfies (a) to (c), condition (b) is relaxed to the cell of the tour's LONGEST leg only (the weights then come from that cell; availability and the portal class still apply). The relaxation is counted per tour and reported with its rate (`n_draws_relaxed_to_longest_leg`, `share_draws_relaxed_to_longest_leg`, INFO), so it is not silent; when even the relaxed set is empty the draw raises, naming person and tour, for a tour without a portal leg (the committed table has pt above `min_share` in every cell, so this cannot happen with it; portal tours follow ruling R26 below). After the draw the stage counts the outside legs whose FINAL mode is still below `min_share` in their cell (`n_outside_legs_implausible_after`, post-condition) and logs a WARNING when that count is above zero; only relaxed draws can leave such legs. Availability follows `braunschweig.synthesis.portal_trips.modes.mode_availability`: car needs car availability and a licence, bicycle needs bicycle availability, car passenger needs `car_passenger_availability` in {some, all}, pt and walk are always available. The draw uses `numpy.random.RandomState(random_seed + 400000)` (the offset is disjoint from 100000 in-commuters, 200000 students, 300000 portal) in `person_id, tour` order. The substitute replaces the mode of EVERY leg of the tour, so vehicle continuity holds and no vehicle is left at a gate; legs whose donor mode was plausible are swept along and counted.
   - **Portal gate class (ruling R17).** The portal stage puts pt stays at rail exit stations and every other mode at road gates (ADR-0141). A tour that contains a portal leg therefore keeps the mode class of its gate: when the current mode of its portal legs is pt the candidates are restricted to pt, otherwise pt is removed from them. Without the rule a re-moded road-gate tour could become a pt tour whose stay sits at a road gate, and a rail-gate tour could become a car tour at a rail station. The portal legs are read from the flag column `portal_leg` that `braunschweig.synthesis.portal_trips.rewrite` writes into `trips.final` (`PORTAL_LEG_COLUMN`, imported from there; the module and its `config_keys` are therefore part of the validation token). A frame without the column is treated as having no portal legs: the report states `portal_leg_column_present`, and the log warns when outside activities exist although the column is absent. The restriction is applied before the relaxation, which must not break it. **A portal tour never aborts the run (ruling R26).** When the gate-class restriction leaves no candidate (in the strict step and after the relaxation), or when the portal legs of the tour sit at both gate classes (one mode per tour cannot serve a rail gate and a road gate), the tour keeps its donor modes unchanged. Realistic triggers: a tour with a pt stay and a road stay, and a road-gate tour of a person who may use only pt and walk (pt is removed by the class, walk is implausible in the portal cell). The tour is counted as `n_portal_tours_left_unchanged` with `portal_tours_left_unchanged_by_reason` (`mixed_gate_class`, `no_candidate_in_gate_class`) and `share_portal_tours_left_unchanged`, and logged at WARNING with count and rate; its implausible legs then appear in `n_outside_legs_implausible_after`, which stays as the post-condition. Without this exception one such tour would abort a 100 % run after the caches are built, and a wrong mode at the gate would be the only alternative, so the donor modes stay and the effect is reported. Non-portal tours keep the raise on an empty set. Reported: `n_portal_tours_remoded` and `n_portal_tours_class_restricted` (the portal tours in which the class removed at least one otherwise admissible candidate).
   - **Unknown donor modes (ruling R18).** An outside leg whose donor mode is none of the five table modes has no table share (0.0), is therefore judged implausible and re-moded; it is counted (`n_unknown_donor_mode`, `unknown_donor_modes` by mode) and logged at WARNING, like the portal stage does for its donor modes.
   - The loader asserts at start that every cell has at least one always-available mode (pt or walk) at `min_share`; an empty candidate set at run time raises, never falls back silently.
   - Walk beyond 5 km is BELOW 1 % for work and education, NOT zero (ruling R10): work 5_10 0.001604 (3 unweighted trips), work 45_plus 0.001412 (1) and education 5_10 0.000792 (1); every other walk cell of these two groups from 5 km is 0.0. The first probe counted only trips TO work and education and found no walk beyond 5 km; the committed table also counts home returns and the business and other-education trips. For the rule the difference is immaterial, because walk only has to be implausible, and it is. For the group `other` the walk share in 5_10 is 0.010481 (36 unweighted trips), which is above the default 1 %: a secondary walk leg of 5 to 10 km is therefore plausible at the default and is not re-moded. In `all` the walk share is below 1 % in every band from 5 km (at most 0.006809), which covers the portal legs.
4. **Stage and position.** `braunschweig.synthesis.outside_tour_modes.stage` (package `braunschweig/synthesis/outside_tour_modes/`: `config_keys.py`, `srv_table.py`, `tours.py`, `rule.py`, `stage.py`) reads `synthesis.population.trips.final` (the reporting day after the portal rewrite), the realised locations `synthesis.population.spatial.locations`, the enriched persons `synthesis.population.enriched` (availability), `data.spatial.municipalities` with `cordon_network_buffer_fraction` (the cut extent, built with `build_cordon_polygon(..., buffer_m_from_fraction(...))` as the scenario cutter does), `data_path` (the table) and `random_seed`. It returns the trips frame with the order, the index and the columns of its input; only `mode` values of re-moded tours change. `configure` raises when the flag is on and `cordon_enabled` is false (no cut extent, nothing to judge), and `remode_outside_tours` raises on a CRS mismatch between locations and municipalities, on a person without an availability row, on a leg without a location and on a categorical `mode` column (ruling R19: the substitutes are assigned as strings and a silent conversion could hide a dtype problem upstream; the caller converts).
5. **The alias, and what reads it.** The DAG node is the alias `synthesis.population.trips.simulated`, defined in the `aliases` section of `configs/base_bs.yml` (the DAG snapshots name the alias, not the stage module, as for `synthesis.output`; the stage semantics therefore live in the Stage Registry record of the alias, ruling R12). Exactly two consumers switch from `synthesis.population.trips.final` to it: `braunschweig.matsim.scenario.population` and `braunschweig.synthesis.commute_day.output_day` (both `DAY_TRIPS_STAGE`). `output_day.configure` declares the stage through its own `_STAGE_MAP`, because the default map of the day-view context would declare `trips.final` while `execute` reads the alias. The chain solver, the portal stage, `synthesis.population.activities.final` and the analyses keep reading `trips.final`: the stage needs the realised locations, which the chain solver produces, so the opposite read would be a cycle. Every `configs/fixtures/*` config that aliases `trips.final` aliases the new alias to the same target and sets the flag to false.
6. **The validation token covers the code and the data.** `validate()` hashes the helper modules, the portal mode helper, the portal rewrite module (the home of the `portal_leg` column name) with its `config_keys`, and the cross-package cordon module (the closure gate `test_every_source_hashing_stage_hashes_its_whole_import_closure` of ADR-0136 must pass), and, with the flag on, the bytes of the committed table: a regenerated table is data, which no source hash sees, and must recompute the stage and everything downstream (ruling R7).
7. **OFF path.** With `braunschweig.outside_tour_modes.enabled: false`, `configure` declares only the flag and `trips.final`, and `execute` returns the input frame unchanged with no counter, no warning and no report file; the pass-through is pinned by test.
8. **Observability (no silent fallback).** Log tag `[outside_tour_modes]`; the report is also written as `outside_tour_modes_report.json` into the stage's cache directory. Keys: `n_persons`, `n_legs`, `n_tours`, `n_outside_tours`, `n_outside_tours_by_group` (by purpose group of the longest leg), `n_outside_tours_remoded`, `share_outside_tours_remoded`, `substitution_matrix` (`donor_modes->substitute`, where a mixed tour carries its sorted donor-mode set), `n_legs_remoded`, `n_legs_swept`, `n_portal_legs_as_all_group`, `n_draws_restricted_by_availability`, `n_removed_by_availability_by_mode`, `cells_used`, plus the settings, and, from the final-review fixes, `n_outside_legs`, `n_draws_relaxed_to_longest_leg`, `share_draws_relaxed_to_longest_leg`, `n_outside_legs_implausible_after`, `portal_leg_column_present`, `n_portal_tours_remoded`, `n_portal_tours_class_restricted`, `n_portal_tours_left_unchanged`, `portal_tours_left_unchanged_by_reason`, `share_portal_tours_left_unchanged`, `n_unknown_donor_mode` and `unknown_donor_modes` (outside legs, by donor mode). The log is a WARNING when the share of re-moded outside tours exceeds `braunschweig.outside_tour_modes.warn_share` (default 0.5, range (0, 1]), when `n_outside_legs_implausible_after` is above zero, when donor modes unknown to the table occur, when a portal tour is left unchanged and when the `portal_leg` column is absent although outside activities exist; the relaxation rate is logged at INFO. The pooling of the table is in its header.
9. **Config keys.** `braunschweig.outside_tour_modes.enabled`, `.min_share` and `.warn_share`, defined once in `config_keys.py`; the production values live in `configs/base_bs.yml` only. The SrV table path is derived from `data_path`.

### Rejected alternatives

- **Mode-aware primary location assignment** (choosing work and education locations so that the diary mode is plausible). The diary mode is a start value; the discrete mode choice decides the mode in the equilibrium for in-region tours. Binding the location to a start value would distort the location choice for a quantity the simulation revises anyway, and it would not touch the secondary locations (half of the outside tours).
- **MiD self-reported distances as thresholds.** The MiD distances are nationwide and self-reported; its walk tail (q0.99 of 11 km reported) is reporting noise. The regional SrV with GIS-routed lengths is the project's distance reference for primary activities (ADR-0102).
- **A fixed substitution order** (as the portal stage uses for the donor mode of a stay, ADR-0141 decision 5: car to car passenger to pt, car passenger to pt, bicycle to pt). A fixed order maps every implausible mode to one mode regardless of the distance and would collapse the substitutes onto pt; drawing from the cell's observed shares reproduces the regional mix of plausible modes at that distance. The portal order is unchanged, because it answers a different question (the person's availability for the donor mode).
- **A check before the secondary location choice.** It sees only the work and education locations (about half of the outside tours, section Context) and none of the distances the chain solver draws.
- **Relaxing `OutsideFilter` so the mode choice acts on these tours.** That changes eqasim core's guard for the tours that touch the outside, outside the scope of this issue (the Java side is not touched, as in ADR-0141 decision 6), and it was not tested; this decision keeps the mode choice as it is and fixes the start value where it is frozen.
- **Extending the Java cutter** (the timing of the outside activity, deep eqasim-core change). The cutter's timing for car and pt outside stays is measured by the plan check, not changed here.

### Assumptions (stated, not measured)

- ASSUMPTION: `min_share` 1 %. A mode below 1 % of the SrV trips of a (purpose group, band) cell is implausible there. The default is a choice, not a derived value; several cells rest on a handful of unweighted trips, so shares near the threshold are noisy (for example `other` 5_10 walk, 36 trips, 0.0105). The A/B shows the direction of a different value, it does not validate one.
- ASSUMPTION: `warn_share` 50 %. Above it the table and the population disagree structurally and the log warns; the production share of re-moded tours is not known before the run.
- ASSUMPTION: portal legs are judged in the group `all` at the top band `45_plus`. A portal leg's distance is not a trip length the bands describe, and the rewritten trip table no longer carries the stay's own purpose; the number of such legs is counted in the report (`n_portal_legs_as_all_group`).
- ASSUMPTION: the regional table (Braunschweig and Region Braunschweig) applies to the whole population, including residents of Wolfsburg, which the survey does not cover (the table has no Kreis rows).
- ASSUMPTION: the detour factor 1.3 converts the routed GIS length to the straight-line equivalent (the pipeline's `ROUTED_DETOUR_FACTOR`), so the band edges are comparable with the realised straight-line leg distances.
- ASSUMPTION: when no mode is plausible on every leg of a tour (and admissible for the person and the gate), judging plausibility in the cell of the tour's longest leg is the least harmful relaxation: the longest leg dominates the tour's travel time and the late plan ends of #443. It can leave shorter legs of the tour implausible; that is what `n_outside_legs_implausible_after` reports.
- ACCEPTED LIMITATION (ruling R20): the draw uses one sequential `RandomState` in `person_id, tour` order, so a change that adds or removes a re-moded tour shifts the draws of every later tour. The run is reproducible for a fixed input and seed; per-tour seeding is a possible later improvement for sensitivity runs. Also accepted: the substitution-matrix key of a mixed tour carries the sorted donor-mode set, and the availability dictionary is built only for the persons that get a substitute.
- ASSUMPTION: the plausible mode mix of a distance band, as observed on all trips of the region, is the right substitute distribution for a frozen tour; it ignores the specific origin and destination.

## Consequences

- **Cache.** The alias resolves to the new stage, so the stage and everything downstream of it recompute once: `braunschweig.matsim.scenario.population`, `synthesis.output` and the MATSim scenario build. PopulationSim, the chain solver, the portal stage and `trips.final` are upstream and stay cached. A regenerated table recomputes the stage through its token.
- **Results change by design when the flag is on.** The modes of the frozen tours of the outside residents change (in the final iteration of `prod100_20260925` these are 12.4 % of the residents); in-region tours and the in-commuters are untouched. The portal report's `n_stays_by_mode` is the mode before this stage; a walk stay can be re-moded downstream. Success criteria to be measured in the A/B: walk and bicycle legs over the plan-check thresholds in frozen tours, the stuck residents by category, the re-mode share and the substitution matrix of the report. None of this is established yet.
- **Not a validation.** The table is a regional survey reference, and the rule enforces plausibility at the frozen tours only. It says nothing about whether the equilibrium mode shares match observed behaviour, and the convergence of the mode shares in a run is stability, not validation.
- **Fail-early behaviour.** The stage raises at configure without the cordon and on invalid settings; at run time on a CRS mismatch, a leg without a location, a person without an availability row, a table cell without a plausible always-available mode, an empty candidate set of a non-portal tour (also after the relaxation) and a categorical `mode` column; a portal tour with no admissible mode is left unchanged and counted instead (ruling R26); the extractor raises on a delivery that is not Tuesday to Thursday and on an empty band or group.
- **Fallbacks are observable, not silent.** The stage has no fallback path that hides a broken primary method: counts and shares are in the log and in the report, and a high re-mode share warns. The one relaxation (to the longest leg's cell) is counted with its rate, and its after-effect is the post-condition count; the portal tours left unchanged are counted by reason and logged at WARNING.
- **Documentation.** ADR-0145; Stage Registry record `synthesis.population.trips.simulated` (the alias node), updated records `synthesis.population.trips.final`, `matsim.scenario.population`, `synthesis.output`; Feature Registry record `outside_tour_modes` and a note in `portal_trips`; Data Registry record `srv2023_mode_by_distance_band`; contributor note `docs/codebase/notes/outside-tour-modes.md`; the DAG snapshot `docs/registry/dag/production.json`.
