# ADR-0212 · 2026-11-28 · Day-absence cells: students by home area, other adults by age band

- **Status:** active (branch `claude/i140-student-flows-fde844`, Phase 4 of the student layer). Amends [ADR-0110](ADR-0110-general-day-absence-as-a-two-stage-state-draw.md), Decision 1 (state model: the individual stage draws per age band) and Decision 4 (stage inputs).
- **Numbering:** ADR-0212 is the next free id after ADR-0211; the lead re-checked it on 2026-11-28 across all local and remote branches and the open pull requests.
- **Issue:** #140 (student layer). Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-student-commuting-design.md`, revision 3 (A3 "Away from home all day", section 5a item 2, A24, A25). Owner decision of 2026-11-27 (absence by cells, the approval of revision 3, section 10 item 2).
- **Numbers:** none are copied here. The rates are in [`srv2023_absence_by_age_band.csv`](../../eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_absence_by_age_band.csv) (columns `p_absent`, `p_absent_non_student`), [`srv2023_student_parameters_by_area.csv`](../../eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_student_parameters_by_area.csv) and [`srv2023_student_area_rule.csv`](../../eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_student_area_rule.csv); config defaults are in the student flows block of `configs/base_bs.yml`.

## Context

ADR-0110 draws the individual residual per AGE BAND at the band's SrV rate of all persons. The student layer needs the students' absence by home area: the day factor of the student balance, `f(k) = e x (1 - a(area(k)))` (design 5a), assumes that the day-absence step takes away `a(area)` of the students of each home area, and the in-commuters count with the same `a`. SrV measures the students' share away from home all day by area (the row `day, away_all_day, students_all, yes` of `srv2023_student_parameters_by_area.csv`); the area rule of Phase 3 gives this parameter a value per area (row `away_all_day` of `srv2023_student_area_rule.csv`: `area_value` True). The band 18 to 29 mixes students and non-students: its rate of all persons (`p_absent`) differs from the rate among its non-students (`p_absent_non_student`, added to `srv2023_absence_by_age_band.csv` by Phase 4); in the older bands the two columns hardly differ.

## Decision

1. **Cells.** The individual stage runs per cell (`braunschweig.synthesis.day_absence.cells`):
   - `student:<area>` for a person aged 18 or older with `P_TAET == STUDENT_P_TAET`, by the home area of the household's Kreis (Braunschweig; the surveyed surrounding Kreise; Wolfsburg, which SrV does not survey, takes the value of the region, [ADR-0205](ADR-0205-one-wolfsburg-rule-for-the-srv-anchored-kreis-targets.md) and design A21); rate: the SrV away share of the area;
   - `adult:<band>` for every other adult with a readable `P_TAET`; rate: `p_absent_non_student` of the band, with the raw SrV weights (A24);
   - `<band>` for a person under 18, rate `p_absent`, unchanged;
   - **an adult of unknown status is drawn at the band's all-person rate** (`P_TAET` missing or the no-answer code): an unknown status is never read as "not a student" (`braunschweig.calibration.mid_student_reference`), so the band's rate of all persons is the expected rate of such an adult. Such an adult goes to the cell `<band>`.
   The rate of an adult band as a whole is no longer a target: it follows from the students and the others the population holds and is reported (`by_band`), not guarded.
2. **Draw unchanged otherwise.** The household stage by household size; the two uniform draw vectors in the same order for all persons; the eligibility gates of ADR-0110 Amendments 1 and 2; the choice of the residual formula; the overshoot and unreachable warnings, now per cell. A cell that holds the persons of a band at the band's rate reproduces the band draw bit for bit (tested), so the persons under 18 keep exactly their state. Under the production settings (minimum household size, escort protection: keys in `configs/base_bs.yml`) a student living alone is reached by the household stage only; the cell rate is reached through the students in larger households (A25), and a cell whose eligible pool cannot carry its rate warns (`n_cells_unreachable`).
3. **Stage.** Switch `day_absence_student_cells_enabled`, default on. On the cells path the stage declares `synthesis.population.spatial.home.zones` (home Kreis through `student_flows_inputs.home_kreis_by_household`) and the Phase 3 home-area key, and requires `P_TAET` in the enriched persons (refused, naming the switch). The deviation guard (`day_absence_max_band_deviation_pp`, `MIN_PERSONS_FOR_BAND_GUARD` in `absence_stage.py`) runs per cell. The adults without a readable `P_TAET` are counted in the diagnostics (`n_adults_unreadable_p_taet`, its share) and logged as a rate; `day_absence_cells_max_unreadable_p_taet_share` (ASSUMPTION, a guard against a broken column; default in `configs/base_bs.yml`) warns above it, and no readable code at all raises. The frame gains `absence_cell`. One reader of `srv2023_absence_by_age_band.csv` (`absence.read_band_rates`) serves both reference loaders.
4. **Token.** ON: the cells helpers and the CRLF-neutral digests of the three SrV tables the cells read enter the stage token. OFF: the module list of the code before the switch.

## Rejected options

- **One rate per adult band (ADR-0110 as it stood).** The students would be thinned at the rate of their band while the balance and the in-commuters use `a(area)`, so the students of the day per site would not add up (design 5a item 2: the model's students before Phase 4 are away at the band's rate). Rejected.
- **Students at the band rate, only the other adults at the non-student rate.** The same inconsistency for the students; and the difference of the students' away share between the two areas passes the area rule (`srv2023_student_area_rule.csv`, row `away_all_day`, columns `difference_in_se` and `area_value`), so one rate for both areas is not what SrV measures. Rejected.
- **Cells per Kreis.** A Kreis cell would hold only a part of the base of the surrounding area (column `n_base_surrounding` of the `away_all_day` row), and the area rule asks for a minimum number of respondents per share (design A14 and A20, [ADR-0211](ADR-0211-students-lower-the-employed-target-and-get-their-own-education-entries.md) Decision 2); with the base split over several Kreise most cells would fall below it. The committed table holds no base per Kreis and none was computed for this record. The areas are the finest split the table supports. Rejected.

## Consequences

- The draw now depends on `P_TAET` and the home Kreis, and the stage gains the edge home zones to day absence on the cells path. Production outputs change: the students by area, the non-students at their own rate. The OFF path (`day_absence_student_cells_enabled: false`) is byte-identical to the code before (tested against stored digests of the output of commit `c12cb6d9`).
- **OFF token:** the module list of the OFF token is unchanged, but the semantic digest of `braunschweig/synthesis/day_absence/absence.py` changed and the new switch is declared on both paths, so a cached day-absence output is recomputed once even with the switch off; the recomputed output is identical.
- Diagnostics on the cells path: `by_cell`, `n_cells_overshoot`, `n_cells_unreachable`, `n_cell_guard_hits`, `area_of_kreis`, `n_adults`, `n_adults_unreadable_p_taet`, `share_adults_unreadable_p_taet`; `by_band` stays as a report.
- With the day absence on and the cells off, students are thinned at their band rate; the student flows refuse that configuration ([ADR-0213](ADR-0213-students-are-placed-by-the-enrollment-balance.md); design 5a item 2).

## Assumptions

A24 (raw SrV weights for the non-students' rate; no school-degree correction outside the ages 20 to 29), A25 (within a cell a student with an education leg is away as often as one without; under the production settings the draw is not independent of the plan and the household), and the warn share of the unreadable `P_TAET` (a guard, not a target).
