## Context

The model's students and the model's employment disagree in the population (design section 2). The employment grid takes the Zensus count of the employed, which includes students with a job, while the seed counts a student as never employed, so students fit only into the non-employed places. The hard control `education_18plus` fixes the adults with an education leg from SrV, a different source from the student level (ADR-0210, Decision 5), so the non-students fill whatever the students leave over. A student whose own diary cannot be used gets a re-drawn plan whose match knows no student status. The measurement by home area shows the students of the city and of the surrounding Kreise differ in some properties and not in others.

## Decision

1. **A2: the employed target is lowered by the students counted as employed (amends ADR-0203, 2026-11-18, for students).** For the grid groups 16 to 29 and 30 to 39 the employed target per Kreis and sex is the Zensus employed count minus the students counted as employed (level table, level times `omega`; the level table's groups are mapped to the grid groups per A13). The subtraction happens at one entry point, `employment_grid.per_cell_employment_targets`; the Kreis control `employed` follows the grid sum and the Kreis families follow through the unchanged bridge (`employment_level.reconcile_kreis_employment_levels`). A negative target is an error. Owner approval 2026-11-26. Switch `braunschweig.population.popsim.student_employment_concept`, default on. The refusals and the two warnings (employment grid off, student education switch off) are listed in the docstring of `_read_student_employment_config` in `braunschweig/popsim/stage/__init__.py`, the one owner; a run without the employment grid has no grid target to lower, so there the concept is inactive with a warning, not refused (lead decision of 2026-11-27).
2. **A3: two hard entries by student status replace `education_18plus` (amends ADR-0109 for the adult band).** The entries cover the age ranges 18 to 29 and 30 or older and share ONE switch, `braunschweig.population.popsim.student_education_kreis_control` (default on); with it on `education_18plus` is dropped by name and the other bands of the education-by-age family stay. The number of adults with an education leg is no longer a target of its own: it is the sum of the students' and the other adults' targets.
   - *Students:* one rounded student count per Kreis and age range (the labels of every entry add up to it), from the level table; the 18 to 29 entry splits the count by one regional SrV share of students with an education leg (design 5a, `education_leg_share_control_definition`), the older range does not split it (A22).
   - *Other adults:* each Kreis keeps its own SrV share of non-student adults with an education leg, split over the two age ranges by the regional pattern of non-students ([`srv2023_education_leg_by_student_status.csv`](../../eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_education_leg_by_student_status.csv), `group == "other_adult"`); Wolfsburg takes the region value (the Wolfsburg rule, [ADR-0205](ADR-0205-one-wolfsburg-rule-for-the-srv-anchored-kreis-targets.md)).
   - *Soft entries:* a student work-leg entry (regional share, A23; the control definition is `E_ZWECK_9` work, the definition of the `work_by_employment` target, lead decision 2 of the plan, header key `work_leg_share_control_definition`) and a student season-ticket entry by HOME AREA (the area rule below; Wolfsburg takes the region value, A21). Each has its own switch, default on.
   - *Refusals:* every contradictory switch combination of the student entries is refused at config time, and the batch split by the population share at the start of the stage. The rules are listed in the docstrings of `source_resolution._apply_student_entry_rules` and `KreisConsistencySettings.require_run_support` (`braunschweig/popsim/stage/`), the one owner.
   - *Guard pairs*: the two hard entries each partition their age range, so two exact age-range pairs replace the 18-plus pair; two at-most pairs are checked per Kreis part AFTER the bridge (students with a work leg against the non-employed persons with a work leg; students with a flat-rate ticket against the holders of `pt_ticket_group4`, reported only). The pair set follows the active entries, so OFF runs write no new rows.
   - *Two rules:* rule 1 (whether a property gets a control: model differs from SrV by more than two standard errors in a cell of at least 30 respondents, and the property changes travel demand) is unchanged; rule 2 (whether the target differs by home area: SrV shares of the city and the surrounding Kreise differ by more than two standard errors, each share on at least 100 respondents with at least 10 on either side) is new. The thresholds are ASSUMPTIONS A14 and A20 of the design (constants in `braunschweig/calibration/student_control_evidence.py`); the result of rule 2 is in the area-rule table linked above.
3. **Amends ADR-0210, Consequences.** (a) The day factor is no longer one regional value: `e` (the education-leg share) stays regional, the away-from-home share is by home area; Phase 4 builds the absence cells, the owner decision of 2026-11-27 (design A3, section 5a). (b) The student control keeps two age groups (18 to 29, 30 or older); the four groups of the level table feed the employed target of Decision 1 only, summed per grid group.
4. **A1: a re-drawn student plan comes from a student.** `student` is `P_TAET == 10`, the code `attributes.STUDENT_TAET`. The key binds STUDENT targets only: a household with a student aged 18 or older (household key `has_student`, first in the soft keys, relaxed last) and a student person (person key `student`, first in the person keys); every other target keeps today's candidate set and every call still consumes one draw. The guarantee "every non-student keeps exactly today's donor" holds PER CALL and per pass on a clean fixture; it does NOT hold for a whole run, because the draws on the shared completion stream depend on which donor a student household gets (unfillable donors leave surplus members, weekend members are caught by the sweep, the diary pass decides on a remap draw); a separate stream would not help because the draw count of the main stream still moves (lead decision 1 of the plan). A student target in a re-drawn household is paired first with a free student of its age band and sex, then of its age band, then with any free student, before the present ladder. The [smoke manifest](../runs/student-synthesis-smoke-20261127.yml) records, at one code state with the key off and on, the student-donor share per path and the share of non-student persons whose re-drawn plan comes from another draw (entry "A1 at one code state"). Each match path counts primary (student donor) and fallback (non-student donor) with the rate in the run info; an always-on warning fires at a 0 % student-donor share (a broken primary path), and a configurable bound `donor_match_student_fallback_warn_share` warns on a high fallback share. The bound is volatile (it steers a warning only and changes no donor); its value in `configs/base_bs.yml` was set after the re-measurement, see the smoke manifest. Switch `braunschweig.population.popsim.donor_match_student_status`, default on.

## Rejected options

All from design section 5 (the trade-offs and the evidence are there):
- **"Student level" table.** A soft student control next to the unchanged employment controls cannot work: the employed places are fixed by the Zensus count and a seed student is never employed. Marking a share of seed students as employed would invent a flag per person that drives the work place assignment and the hard employment key of the plan match. Chosen: Decision 1.
- **"Area and age detail" table.** (A) One regional share for every Kreis keeps the error of the non-students' education legs and misstates the ticket share and the away share by area. (C) Own SrV values per Kreis with hard age-and-sex cells per Kreis would fix sampling noise (few respondents per Kreis outside the city) and small hard cells in every batch. Chosen: (B) an area value only where rule 2 finds a difference, one regional value otherwise (owner decision 2026-11-27).
- **"Adults with an education leg" table.** Keeping the hard SrV total and adding the student control leaves the non-students the remainder, which the committed measurement shows for the city only (Kreis 03101 beyond two SrV standard errors); the other Kreise lie within about 1.3 standard errors with mixed signs (`education_leg_by_student_status.csv` of the [by-area manifest](../runs/student-measurement-by-area-popsim100-20261120-20261127.yml)). Keeping the total and raising the students' share until it fills would need an education-leg share of students above the SrV share and put more persons at every site than the balance holds. Chosen: margin times conditional share for both groups, the total follows (the pattern of `work_by_employment`, ADR-0109).

## Consequences

- **OFF paths:** with a switch off, the targets are byte-identical to today's (the existing byte-identity tests pin it). The stage cache token changes once for every run, OFF runs included, because the PopulationSim stage gains new modules and reads the level table among its committed files; the stage report always gains a `student_employment` entry, even with the switch off. This re-runs the cache only.
- **Effective with the owner's next full run;** no production PopulationSim run belongs to this branch.
- **Not controlled:** "lives with a person at least 18 years older" and car access. The measurement by area ([manifest](../runs/student-measurement-by-area-popsim100-20261120-20261127.yml), `student_behaviour_by_area.csv`) shows the model beyond two SrV standard errors under rule 1 for "lives with an older person" in the city and in the surrounding Kreise, and for car access only in the surrounding Kreise (the city is within one standard error); adding a soft entry is an owner decision, pending. Nothing is added without the owner's yes.
- The employed target and the student entries agree in every batch part to first order because both are split by the young persons of a cell; the grid group starts at 16, the entry at 18.
- Evidence of the bundle is a one-batch smoke, not a validation: it measures fit, donors, weight dispersion and the students by household type against SrV by area. The smoke manifest: [student-synthesis-smoke-20261127](../runs/student-synthesis-smoke-20261127.yml).
