# ADR-0213 · 2026-11-28 · Students are placed by the enrollment balance

- **Status:** active (branch `claude/i140-student-flows-fde844`, Phase 4 of the student layer). Amends [ADR-0157](ADR-0157-adults-choose-their-education-location-among-all-education-facilities-by-distance.md) (adults choose among all education facilities: on the ON path the age threshold no longer decides) and [ADR-0024](ADR-0024-education-gravity-real-schools-kita-university.md) (the university step: a singly-constrained decay is replaced by a balanced assignment). ## Context

Everything rests on one table of head counts, T(k, j): the students of site j who live in Kreis k (ADR-0210, design 5a). Before this decision the model's students did not follow it: the location stage routed persons by age alone (every person aged 20 or older to a university, ADR-0024; from a configured age on to any education facility, ADR-0157), placed the residents' university students by independent draws of a decay, and counted the in-commuters as the enrollment of a commune minus the residents placed, with origins drawn by a reverse decay (`docs/features/student-incommuters.md`, OFF path). A site's total then depended on the population's error and on a decay slope fitted to a national mean distance, and the origins of T were not used.

## Decision

1. **B1: routing by status, not by age.** With `braunschweig.students.flows_enabled` (default on) a person with an education activity is routed by the survey status `P_TAET` (`braunschweig/synthesis/locations/education_routing.py`):
   - a student aged 18 or older goes to the university level, at every adult age;
   - a student under 18 and a non-student under 20 keep today's age bands (counted);
   - an apprentice or pupil (`P_TAET` 8 or 9) aged 20 or older goes to the vocational schools (`bbs_adult`): a singly-constrained decay over the `bbs` schools with their capacities as weights, one scalar slope, the `bbs` entry of `education_gravity_slope_by_level`; the per-RS7 slope of the pupils' placement is not used, because adult apprentices and pupils are few and `assign_by_decay` takes one slope (ASSUMPTION);
   - everyone else aged 20 or older follows the adult-education rule of ADR-0157 among the NDS schools, WITHOUT university buildings; `education_adult_min_age` no longer decides on this path (amends ADR-0157, Decisions 1 to 3, for the ON path);
   - a person whose status is unknown (no `P_TAET` in the population, or a missing or no-answer code) is never read as "not a student": the fallback `studies and not employed and age >= 20` decides per person, counted and logged as a rate, with a warn share (`braunschweig.students.status_fallback_warn_share`).
   The vocational schools' capacities count adult pupils; the record `lsn_schools` states what is known about it.
2. **B2: facilities per destination of T.** `build_site_facilities` (`braunschweig/data/schools/university_facilities.py`) builds one facility set per destination of T from the committed tables, not from the local-only institution file. A local site spreads its enrollment over the university buildings of its commune; a surrounding institution is one facility at its point of `student_destination_points.csv`, the point T's distances were measured to. Two sites in one commune (TU Braunschweig and the art academy) both get all university buildings of the commune with capacities by their own enrollment, because the building data do not say which building belongs to whom (ASSUMPTION). A destination without any student of the region has no share in T: it is left out and counted; buildings of a commune without a site are left out, counted and logged as a rate with a warn share (`braunschweig.students.university_left_out_warn_share`). A destination of T without a facility otherwise, and a local site without a university building or without enrollment, raise.
3. **B5: the residents' balanced assignment** (`braunschweig/synthesis/locations/university_assignment.py`, called by `education_gravity`):
   - **Fit.** The flow matrix home Kreis x destination is fitted (iterative proportional fitting from the heads of T) to the persons of level `university` each home Kreis has PRESENT on the simulated day (rows) and to each destination's share of the region's heads in T (columns, summing to the rows). The location stage therefore reads the state the day-absence step drew, an input declared only on this path. The fit stops at a configured tolerance and raises when it does not converge (`braunschweig.students.fit_tolerance`, `fit_max_iterations`).
   - **Rounding.** Unbiased controlled rounding (Cox 1987; the cycle-and-path step is the dependent rounding of Gandhi et al. 2006): every cell, row sum, column sum and the total become the floor or the ceiling of their real values, and every cell keeps its value in expectation. A home Kreis without a row of T takes the legacy decay (counted and logged as a rate, a warning on any use); it must be none on the committed table.
   - **Allotment inside a home Kreis by distance, not at random** (owner decision of 2026-11-27, in its most accurate feasible form). Rule `plan_length`: each person gets one destination of the Kreis's row so that every destination receives exactly its cell total and the summed difference between the straight line home to destination and the length of the person's own education leg is smallest; only an observed leg counts, never an imputed one. Rule `home_distance`: the same with the distance from the home alone. Both rules are built behind `braunschweig.students.kreis_matching_rule`. A person without an observed leg is the counted fallback: placed on places held back for such persons, one after the other in a seeded order, by the decay of `education_university_slope` weighted by the held-back places left (the capacity analogue of the decay of the OFF path); a share above `braunschweig.students.matching_fallback_warn_share` warns. The remaining persons are matched to the remaining places by an exact minimum-cost allotment solved in the module (successive shortest paths, with a final check for a negative cycle). A building of the chosen site is then drawn in proportion to capacity.
   - **Default rule.** `plan_length`, pending the measurement against the SrV distance table that the design fixed beforehand (design B5: the rule closer to SrV becomes the default; inside the noise floor the plan rule stays, because it keeps plan and place consistent). **[MARKED FOR THE LEAD: replace this sentence with the measured default and the link to the run manifest once the rule comparison has run.]**
   - **Absent persons** of level `university` follow the same steps on their own whole-numbered totals (the fitted row shares of their Kreis), counted apart and marked by an `absent_` path; they do not travel on the simulated day. A Kreis with absent persons but nobody present has no fitted row: its absent persons take the shares of their row of T (counted, a warning).
4. **B6: the in-commuters** (`braunschweig/synthesis/student_incommuters.py`): per origin Kreis outside the region and site, the count is T x the effective day factor x the sampling rate, rounded so that every site and every origin Kreis keeps its total within one person (the same controlled rounding). The effective day factor is the mean of the residents' factors weighted by the students of each Kreis in the level table, from the committed SrV tables and the away shares the run applies (cells on: by home area, ADR-0212; day absence off: 0); the flows are refused with the day absence on and its student cells off. The in-commuters join the scenario after the day-absence step and are not drawn by it. Each agent carries its destination of T and a building of the site drawn by capacity; its home follows the existing rule (a population-weighted point inside the ring, else the nearest cordon gate; `home_branch` records the branch per agent); its commute length for the donor window and the mode band is the one T implies, not the one of the home point: the straight line from a commune of the origin Kreis, drawn by population times the weight curve of T, to the site's point, times `braunschweig.constants.ROUTED_DETOUR_FACTOR`, with the distance of the cell in T as the counted fallback (`braunschweig.student_incommuters.commute_length_fallback_warn_share`). The mode, car or public transport, comes from the students' mode table by distance band of the routed length (`braunschweig/data/education/student_mode_reference.py`), with a thin band pooled with its neighbour (minimum `braunschweig.student_incommuters.mode_band_min_persons`, ASSUMPTION: the table states counts, not a minimum).
5. **B6a: the out-commuting students are measured, not changed.** The mechanisms for a leg out of the region (the scenario cut, the rewrite of a leg beyond the routable distance into a stay at a gate, the mode rule of `outside_tour_modes`) are not touched. The analysis stage `student_out_commuters` reports their modes by distance band and the legs rewritten at a gate; the students whose education leg it cannot identify with certainty are counted and the blind spot is stated in its module docstring. A rule of their own for the out-commuters' modes is open to the owner (design section 10).
6. **Switches, diagnostics.** One switch for B1 to B6, `braunschweig.students.flows_enabled`, default on; off keeps the behaviour before the flows. The analysis stage `student_flows` writes the acceptance table with its rule (a row of at least a configured size whose deviation exceeds a configured share fails the verdict; both ASSUMPTIONS A17) and the diagnostics of the flows; the definitions are in its module docstring. Every fallback is logged as `[tag] primary n/N (x%), fallback m (y%)`.

## Rejected options

- **The residual count (enrollment x sampling rate minus the residents placed, floored at 0) for the in-commuters.** It ties a site's in-commuters to the error of the residents' placement and ignores the origins of T. Kept as the OFF path only.
- **The reverse decay for the origins.** Its origins come from a decay slope fitted to a national mean distance, not from the origins of T (the CHE counts with the owner's commuter weights, ADR-0210). Kept as the OFF path only.
- **A random allotment inside a home Kreis** (the approved text of B5 until the review of Phase 4). A random draw would send a student who lives next to a campus to another town as readily as one who lives at the other end of the Kreis, and the decay it replaces is sensitive to that distance, so it would be a step back. The analysis stage reports the mean distance of the allotment next to that of a random allotment.
- **A general solver for the allotment (scipy `linprog` with HiGHS, or networkx).** Too slow at the size of the largest home Kreis: the dated measurement of HiGHS is in the module docstring of `braunschweig/synthesis/locations/university_assignment.py` (ASSUMPTION there); the networkx run of the build did not finish at that size and is not recorded in the code. The module's own exact solver is cross-checked against HiGHS on small random cases in its tests.
- **Keeping `education_adult_min_age` as the router for students.** A student aged 30 or older would be sent to any education facility instead of a university; the status is what the survey records and what the level table counts (its age groups include students of 30 or older, ADR-0211).

## Consequences

- **Output change by design** on the ON path: the students' places follow T, adults aged 20 or older without a student status leave the university buildings for the school buildings (the analysis stage counts them), and the in-commuters change in number, origin, commute length and mode. No figure of that change is stated here; the run manifests of issue #140 hold the measurements. The flows are a measurement of the synthesis, not a validation: T rests on the CHE origins and the owner's weights, and in-commuting is observed nowhere.
- **OFF path** (`flows_enabled: false`): byte-identical output of each changed stage to the code before (one frame-equal test per stage). The stage cache hash changes once for every run, OFF runs included, because the stages gain modules and keys.
- **New inputs** of `education_gravity` on the ON path: the trips (the observed education leg), the home zones, and with the day absence on the day-absence stage. The stage graph is checked separately.
- **Assumptions:** the status fallback; the scalar `bbs_adult` slope; the shared buildings of two sites in one commune; the decay fallback of the allotment, with the slope of the OFF path (A26); a student with an education leg is away as often as one without (A25, ADR-0212); the minimum of the mode bands; every warn share and the acceptance thresholds (config comments). The sites' total of the day is the enrollment times the effective day factor only to the extent the population meets the level table (ADR-0211).
- **Open:** the default matching rule (Decision 3, the marked sentence); the rule for the out-commuters' modes; the vocational capacities' overlap (record `lsn_schools`).
