## Context

Everything rests on one table of head counts, T(k, j): the students of site j who live in Kreis k (ADR-0210; design, section 5a "The quantities: one balance"). Before this decision the model's students did not follow it: the location stage routed persons by age alone (every person aged 20 or older to a university, ADR-0024; from a configured age on to any education facility, ADR-0157), placed the residents' university students by independent draws of a decay, and counted the in-commuters as the enrollment of a commune minus the residents placed, with origins drawn by a reverse decay (OFF path, `docs/features/student-incommuters.md`). A site's total then depended on the population's error and on a decay slope fitted to a national mean distance, and the origins of T were not used.

## Decision

1. **Routing by status, not by age** (design, B1 "Student status in the location stage"). With `braunschweig.students.flows_enabled` (default on) a person with an education activity is routed by the survey status `P_TAET` (`braunschweig/synthesis/locations/education_routing.py`):
   - a student aged 18 or older goes to the university level, at every adult age;
   - a student under 18 and a non-student under 20 keep today's age bands (counted);
   - an apprentice or pupil (`P_TAET` 8 or 9) aged 20 or older goes to the vocational schools, drawn by a singly-constrained decay with the schools' capacities as weights and one scalar slope, the `bbs` entry of `education_gravity_slope_by_level`; the per-RS7 slope of the pupils' placement is not used (ASSUMPTION: adult apprentices and pupils are few, and the decay helper takes one slope);
   - everyone else aged 20 or older follows the adult-education rule of ADR-0157 among the NDS schools, WITHOUT university buildings; `education_adult_min_age` no longer decides on this path (amends ADR-0157 for the ON path);
   - a person whose status is unknown is never read as "not a student": a fallback rule decides per person, counted and logged as a rate with a warn share.
   The vocational schools' capacities count adult pupils; the record `lsn_schools` states what is known about it.
2. **Facilities per destination of T** (design, B2 "Enrollment per site"). `braunschweig/data/schools/university_facilities.py` builds one facility set per destination of T from the committed tables, not from the local-only institution file; a surrounding institution is one facility at its point of `student_destination_points.csv`, the point T's distances were measured to. Two sites in one commune (TU Braunschweig and the art academy) both get all university buildings of the commune with capacities by their own enrollment, because the building data do not say which building belongs to whom (ASSUMPTION). A destination no student of the region attends has no share in T and is left out and counted; a destination of T without a facility otherwise raises.
3. **The residents' balanced assignment** (design, B5 "Residents' choice of university"; `braunschweig/synthesis/locations/university_assignment.py`, called by `education_gravity`; the fit, the rounding, the allotment and the absent persons are described in that module's docstring). The decisions:
   - the flow matrix home Kreis x destination is fitted on the persons PRESENT on the simulated day, so the location stage reads the day-absence state (an input declared only on this path), and the persons are rounded without bias;
   - inside a home Kreis the persons are matched to the destinations by distance, not at random (owner decision of 2026-11-27): by the length of the person's own plan education leg (rule `plan_length`, design assumption A26: the plan says how far a person with this day travels; T fixes how many go where, the matching decides who), with the university decay of the OFF path as the counted fallback for a person without an observed leg; or by the distance from the home alone (rule `home_distance`). Both rules are built behind `braunschweig.students.kreis_matching_rule`;
   - **default rule:** `plan_length`, pending the SrV distance comparison that the design fixed beforehand (the rule closer to SrV becomes the default; inside the noise floor the plan rule stays, because it keeps plan and place consistent). **[MARKED FOR THE LEAD: replace this sentence with the measured default and the link to the run manifest once the rule comparison has run.]**
4. **The in-commuters** (design, B6 "In-commuters"; `braunschweig/synthesis/student_incommuters.py`; method text: `docs/features/student-incommuters.md`). The decisions: the count is T x the effective day factor x the sampling rate, with the margins kept; the effective day factor is the mean of the residents' factors, from the committed SrV tables and the away share the run applies (cells on: by home area, ADR-0212; day absence off: zero); the flows are refused with the day absence on and its cells off; each agent carries its destination of T; its commute length is the one T implies (from a commune of the origin Kreis to the site point), not the one of the home point at the edge of the region; its mode comes from the students' mode table by distance band, with thin bands pooled (ASSUMPTION: the table states counts, not a minimum).
5. **The out-commuting students are measured, not changed** (design, B6a "Out-commuters and long legs: not changed, measured"). The mechanisms for a leg out of the region (the scenario cut, the rewrite of a leg beyond the routable distance into a stay at a gate, the mode rule of `outside_tour_modes`) are not touched. The analysis stage `student_out_commuters` reports their modes by distance band and the legs rewritten at a gate; the students whose education leg it cannot identify with certainty are counted, and the blind spot is stated in its module docstring. A rule of their own for the out-commuters' modes is open to the owner.
6. **Switch and diagnostics.** One switch for Decisions 1 to 4, `braunschweig.students.flows_enabled`, default on; off keeps the behaviour before the flows. The analysis stage `student_flows` writes the acceptance table with its rule and the diagnostics of the flows (definitions in its module docstring). Every fallback is counted and logged as a rate with primary and fallback counts.

## Rejected options

- **The residual count (enrollment x sampling rate minus the residents placed) for the in-commuters.** It ties a site's in-commuters to the error of the residents' placement and ignores the origins of T. Kept as the OFF path only.
- **The reverse decay for the origins.** Its origins come from a decay slope fitted to a national mean distance, not from the origins of T (the CHE counts with the owner's commuter weights, ADR-0210). Kept as the OFF path only.
- **A random allotment inside a home Kreis** (the text of design B5 until the review of Phase 4). A random draw would send a student who lives next to a campus to another town as readily as one who lives at the other end of the Kreis, and the decay it replaces is sensitive to that distance, so it would be a step back. The analysis stage reports the mean distance of the allotment next to that of a random allotment.
- **A general solver for the allotment (scipy `linprog` with HiGHS).** Too slow: a dated single measurement on the development laptop at 1,000 and 2,000 persons is in the docstring of `braunschweig/synthesis/locations/university_assignment.py`, with the size of the largest home Kreis stated there as an ASSUMPTION; no figure for that size is claimed here. The module solves the transport problem itself.
- **Keeping `education_adult_min_age` as the router for students.** A student aged 30 or older would be sent to any education facility instead of a university; the status is what the survey records and what the level table counts (its age groups include students of 30 or older, ADR-0211).

## Consequences

- **Output change by design** on the ON path: the students' places follow T, adults aged 20 or older without a student status leave the university buildings for the school buildings (the analysis stage counts them), and the in-commuters change in number, origin, commute length and mode. No figure of that change is stated here; the run manifests of issue #140 hold the measurements. The flows are a measurement of the synthesis, not a validation: T rests on the CHE origins and the owner's weights, and in-commuting is observed nowhere.
- **OFF path** (`flows_enabled: false`): byte-identical output of each changed stage to the code before (one frame-equal test per stage). The stage cache hash changes once for every run, OFF runs included, because the stages gain modules and keys.
- **New inputs** of `education_gravity` on the ON path: the trips (the observed education leg), the home zones, and with the day absence on the day-absence stage.
- **Assumptions:** the scalar slope of the vocational schools; the shared buildings of two sites in one commune; the decay fallback of the allotment (design assumption A26); a student with an education leg is away as often as one without (design assumption A25, ADR-0212); the pooling minimum of the mode bands; every warn share and the acceptance thresholds (config comments). A site's total of the day is the enrollment times the effective day factor only to the extent the population meets the level table (ADR-0211).
- **Open:** the default matching rule (Decision 3, the marked sentence); the rule for the out-commuters' modes; the overlap of the vocational capacities (record `lsn_schools`).
