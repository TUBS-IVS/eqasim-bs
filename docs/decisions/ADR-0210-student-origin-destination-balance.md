# ADR-0210 · 2026-11-26 · The student origin-destination table uses a commune-level weight curve; the commuter weights are an owner assumption because the calibration could not decide

- **Status:** active (branch `claude/i140-student-balance-3b6cbe`, Phase 2 of the student layer). Decision 5 (the level of the students) was decided by the owner on 2026-11-27.
- **Numbering:** ADR-0210 is the next free id; checked on 2026-11-26 across all branches, the open pull requests and the open issues (ADR-0209 was already taken elsewhere).
- **Issue:** #140 (student layer), #279. Owner decisions of 2026-11-26.
- **Numbers:** every figure of this build is in the run manifests [`docs/runs/student-balance-2026-11-26.yml`](../runs/student-balance-2026-11-26.yml) (calibration, weights decision, level decision aid) and [`docs/runs/student-balance-2026-11-27.yml`](../runs/student-balance-2026-11-27.yml) (the final tables, the gate and the level) and in the headers of the committed tables; this record copies none of them.

## Context

The student layer needs the number of students by the Kreis they live in and by the site they study at (the table T of design B3, counted in heads, never in days). The only source of origins is the CHE ranking (Kreis of the school-leaving certificate), published as two "top 10" lists that show only part of the cells. The weights that turn a CHE origin into a commuter depend on distance and are not observed anywhere; the SrV 2023 destination reference of the surveyed Kreise is the only yardstick.

## Decision

1. **Table rules (B3)**, owned in code by `braunschweig/data/education/student_od_table.py`:
   - A cell is the listed value of either CHE view (per institution, per Kreis); a cell shown in both views with different numbers is an error.
   - A cell shown in neither view is censored: it enters as 0 and carries an upper bound (the smallest listed value of every ten-row list it is missing from). The bounds and the count of cells without a bound are written to the table headers.
   - The art academy has no own CHE list. Its cells of the Kreis view are used (the Braunschweig origin cell; every other origin Kreis is censored); the rest of its LSN enrollment counts as locals, unscaled (scale factor 1, no CHE total).
   - Distance-learning institutions (assumption A7) are dropped and counted; they are not in the balance.
   - In the split of a multi-site institution the site in the origin Kreis itself gets weight 1 (A5, A6).
   - Movers (students who do not live in the origin Kreis, A6) can be moved to Braunschweig by a share at named site Kreise.
2. **Weight rule: a continuous curve evaluated per commune of the home Kreis** (anchors at the band midpoints 17.5, 37.5 and 75 km, 0 from 100 km, the design's maximum commute distance A10), averaged with the communes' population. The population averaging is the owner decision of 2026-11-26 (this record; it has no assumption id). The curve replaces the design's hard bands at the population-weighted Kreis centroid.
3. **Calibration rule: decide or give the range.** Two stages are fitted against the SrV destination reference (weights; weights and movers share). A stage is `decided` only if its optimum lies inside the grid and no parameter ranges widely within the noise floor; otherwise it reports its ranges and writes no configuration value. Grid step, mover step and the maximum flat range are ASSUMPTIONS of the lead, CLI arguments.
4. **Outcome:** neither stage could decide (optimum at the bound, all parameters within the noise floor; reasons in the calibration header). The owner chose option C of the decision aid on 2026-11-26 (a weight triple and a movers share at one site Kreis) and set the values as an ASSUMPTION of the owner. The table headers carry `weights_source=owner`. The values are in the manifest.
5. **Gate and level: decided by the owner on 2026-11-27.** The gate table carries two comparisons with opposite blind spots (rows and flags in the manifests):
   - *Comparison 1, the design's (A2 gate):* the balance against the SrV count of the Kreis, students away all day added. SrV covers only the surveyed municipalities of a Kreis, so this count can understate the Kreis. Flag `gate_triggered`.
   - *Comparison 2, whole Kreis:* the 20-29 part of the balance against the SrV share of the 20-29 year olds times the Zensus population 20-29 of the whole Kreis. It covers the whole Kreis but applies the share of the surveyed municipalities to all of them. Flag `gate_triggered_area_consistent`.
   - The Zensus-based estimate stands in the same table as a third reference. The gate keeps comparing the balance row sums with SrV; `build_level_gate` and both triggers are unchanged.
   - The gate trigger flipped between the two final runs. `gate_triggered` was False in the final run of 2026-11-26 (header of [`student_level_gate.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/student_level_gate.csv)) and is True in the final run of 2026-11-27 (header of [`student_level_gate.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-27/student_level_gate.csv)); the pooled row difference in SrV standard errors is read from each file (column `difference_in_srv_se`, figures in the manifest [`student-balance-2026-11-27`](../runs/student-balance-2026-11-27.yml)). The owner's decision of 2026-11-27 (the level is NOT scaled to SrV) predates that run; per the design (A2) a trigger means the owner decides, and the owner confirmed on 2026-11-27, after the flip was reported, that the level is not scaled.
   - The decision, items 1 to 5 are the owner's package ("Umsetzen, mit der feinen Aufteilung auf der MiD (wenn machbar)"); the items marked DESIGNER are calls of the designer (the lead), recorded as such:
     1. **Enrollment.** The flow table `T` uses winter semester 2025/26 (LSN-Online K3100101, the newest official semester). The three institutions in Sachsen-Anhalt get an enrollment too: Magdeburg university at 2025/26; Magdeburg-Stendal and Harz at 2024/25, their last published winter semester (a counted fallback, rate in the manifest).
     2. **Level per Kreis (assumption A18).** The students living in the eight Kreise in total stay what the balance says (row sums of `T`). Their split over the Kreise follows the census: `level_k = total * Z_k * c_k / sum_j(Z_j * c_j)`, `Z_k` = Zensus 2022 pupils and students without paid work aged 20 to 29.
     3. **Carried to the model year (assumption A19).** `c_k` = persons aged 20 to 29 on 2025-12-31 over those on 2022-12-31 (LSN-Online A100001K).
     4. **omega in one year (A12 rewritten).** `omega = 1 - Z / S` with `S` = students living in the eight Kreise under the enrollment of winter semester 2022/23 times the MiD share of the ages 20 to 29; `T` is built a second time for that. DESIGNER: winter semester 2022/23 stands for the census day (2022-05-15, in summer semester 2022) because the level is a winter-semester stock, the CHE origins are of that semester and all 21 sites of 16 institutions have a published figure.
     5. **Age and sex (A9 rewritten).** Four age groups `18_19`, `20_24`, `25_29`, `30plus`, by sex. A cell is the Kreis level times the JOINT share of (sex, age group) among the adult students of Germany by main activity in MiD 2023. DESIGNER: the joint MiD cell replaces "SrV age share times SrV sex share" (SrV has no joint cell and far fewer respondents; the SrV respondent count is in [`srv2023_student_parameters.csv`](../../eqasim-data/data/braunschweig/srv/srv2023_student_parameters.csv), the MiD counts in `mid2023_student_age_sex.csv`). DESIGNER: every Kreis gets the same mix; the rows `regiostar7_*` of the same MiD table show the mix by RegioStaR 7 type as evidence of what that leaves out.
   - Not changed: the method of `T`, the commuter weights and the mover share (owner decision of 2026-11-26), the day factor, `build_level_gate`, the two gate triggers (they keep comparing the balance row sums with SrV). The inputs of the second comparison did move: its balance side (`balance_students_20_29_persons`) now uses the MiD share of the ages 20 to 29 instead of the SrV share, which accounts for most of the shift of its pooled difference in SrV standard errors (figures in the manifest).


## Rationale for the options

The decision aid [`weight_candidates.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/weights_decision/weight_candidates.csv) scores each candidate against eight SrV yardsticks (columns `z_*`, `sum_squared_z_yardsticks`; the z values rank candidates and are no test statistic) and carries the gate columns.

- **Why not the calibration optima:** both imply a share of education legs of 50 km or more above the SrV share (column `z_distance_50_km_or_more`, both rows positive). The model side of that yardstick is the straight line times the detour factor 1.3 (`circuity.LEGACY_DETOUR_FACTOR`, an ASSUMPTION; sensitivity columns for 1.2 and 1.4), the SrV side is routed. Option C is also above the SrV share, by less. The SrV evidence for movers is the share of students living in Braunschweig who study in Wolfenbüttel: a thin base of few respondents with a gap of little more than one standard error (counts in the manifest), and the calibration did not identify a mover share.
- **Where option C loses:** compared with options A and B, option C is further from SrV on `z_leave_region` and `z_to_region_hannover` (both), on `z_bs_study_in_bs` and `z_bs_leave_region` (than B) and on `z_distance_25_50_km` (than A); on the pooled level (`level_difference_in_srv_se_03ZGB`) it is further than both. It is chosen because it is closer on the 50 km share, the Wolfenbüttel yardstick, `z_to_other_outside` and the summed squares, not because it fits everywhere. The SrV standard errors of the aid are approximate (ASSUMPTION in its header).

## Rejected options

- **Hard bands at the Kreis centroid** (design as first written): not measured how much of a Kreis's population lies in another band than its centroid; no committed table shows it. The only committed evidence is the comparison run: its optimum differs from the commune-curve optimum in both stages, its objective is higher in both stages, and it is also undecided (values in the manifest). Kept as the comparison run `comparison_centroid_bands/` (calibration) and `options/comparison_centroid_bands/` (tables).
- **Option A (`option_a_stage_weights_optimum` in `weight_candidates.csv`):** the weights optimum without movers. Row values: highest `z_distance_50_km_or_more` and highest `sum_squared_z_yardsticks` of the three named options; it triggers the design's gate (`gate_triggered`).
- **Option B (`option_b_stage_weights_and_movers_optimum`):** the weights-and-movers optimum with movers at three site Kreise. Loses to option C on `z_distance_50_km_or_more` and `sum_squared_z_yardsticks`.
- **Scaling the level to SrV** (Decision 5): the SrV sample aged 20 to 29 holds more persons with an entrance qualification than the census, so SrV over-represents students; the counts are in [`srv_school_degree_20_29.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/level_decision/srv_school_degree_20_29.csv). The structural reason: Phase 4 distributes the region's students of the day over the sites in the shares of the balance (design section 5a), so a level above the balance total would put more students at every local site than its official enrollment times the day factor, and the identity "students of a local site = official enrollment" would no longer hold. The level lies between the survey-based estimates (manifest entry "the level between the independent estimates of the students aged 20 to 29" in [`student-balance-2026-11-27`](../runs/student-balance-2026-11-27.yml)); these are comparisons of estimates, not a validation.
- **Keeping the balance's own Kreis split of the level** (Decision 5): the CHE origin is the Kreis of the school of the entrance qualification, not of the residence ([`entrance_qualifications_by_school_district.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/level_decision/entrance_qualifications_by_school_district.csv)), and the comparison with the census pattern, including the sum of squared differences, is in [`student_level_estimates_20_29.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/level_decision/student_level_estimates_20_29.csv).
- **The SrV age shares** (Decision 5): the alternatives and their shares are in [`student_age_distribution.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/level_decision/student_age_distribution.csv); the MiD joint cell replaces them (item 5).
- **The enrollment of 2024/25 or of 2022/23 as the level** (Decision 5): the owner chose the newest official semester for the level (item 1); 2022/23 is used only for omega (item 4). The balance columns of each semester are in [`student_level_estimates_20_29.csv`](../runs/artifacts/student-balance-2026-11-26/level_decision/student_level_estimates_20_29.csv).

## Consequences

- The five committed tables (record `student_od_balance`, `student_level_table`) carry values set by the owner, not calibrated ones. Any reader must treat the weights as an assumption; the SrV reference does not identify them (ranges in the manifest).
- Known limits, with their figures in the manifest: the in-commuters are observed nowhere (only the CHE counts bound them); the balance's pooled row lies below SrV and the cause is not fully measured (candidate explanations: distance learning and institutions outside the choice set are not in the balance; the balance counts enrolled students, SrV counts students by main activity, two errors in opposite directions, A16; SrV coverage of the Kreis); Goslar lies far above SrV, which covers selected municipalities of that Kreis only; the level is decided (Decision 5), its stated uncertainty is in the manifest of 2026-11-27.
- The tables count heads; the day factor is written to the header for Phases 3 and 4 only. The design applies one regional day factor to every Kreis; the day behaviour by area is to be measured in Phase 3 (manifest entry "students aged 20 to 29 with an education leg on the reporting day" in [`student-balance-2026-11-27`](../runs/student-balance-2026-11-27.yml)).
- This is a table build and a calibration on survey aggregates, not a validation.
- The level table differs from the row sums of `T` per Kreis (it keeps their total and splits it by the census), so Phase 4 compares "students per home Kreis" with the level table, not with `T`. Phase 3 gets four age groups (`18_19`, `20_24`, `25_29`, `30plus`).
