than the constant detour factor 1.3 for the ZGB distribution fits: the band-shift EMD delta
(curve vs constant 1.3) is only ~0.003 for commute (vs MiD P13) and ~+0.002 (slightly worse)
for pooled secondary walk (vs MiD W12) -- both far below the 0.01 materiality threshold.
=> The pipeline keeps the **constant 1.3 as the default**; the fitted curve is retained as
opt-in infrastructure (`mode="curve"`). This mirrors the commute-friction outcome: machinery
built, measured, found not warranted (no overfitting).
